# Code of conduct

## Expectations

Everyone is welcome to participate in open-compute, regardless of experience, background, identity, or ability. Treat contributors and users with respect.

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Give specific, constructive feedback and respect differing viewpoints.
- Accept corrections, acknowledge mistakes, and respect others' time and boundaries.
- Respect privacy. Ask permission before sharing personal information or private correspondence.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory insults, sexual harassment, doxxing, and sustained disruption are unacceptable. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces, as well as public interactions where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the project maintainer, [@elliothux](https://github.com/elliothux), at [elliothu.my@gmail.com](mailto:elliothu.my@gmail.com), with the subject `open-compute conduct report`. Include relevant links or context and how you would prefer to be contacted. Avoid posting sensitive details in public threads.

Reports will be handled discreetly. Information will be shared only as needed to investigate, take action, or meet legal obligations. Absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. For violations involving the maintainer or abuse on GitHub, you can also use [GitHub's abuse reporting channels](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove content, request a correction, issue a warning, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently, depending on the conduct, impact, and any repeated violations. Where practical, the person affected will receive an explanation and may reply privately with additional context.

Technical disagreement and good-faith bug or security reports are welcome. This policy applies to conduct, not to whether a contribution is accepted.
